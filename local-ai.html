Privacy boundary: CRM Intelligence never needs your Ollama password or API key. Your Excel/CSV stays in the browser. The AI request goes to localhost:11434 on the same computer.
Model recommendations are conservative application guidance, not official hardware requirements. A larger model can increase RAM/CPU/GPU use, so this wizard intentionally prefers the smallest model likely to be useful for the available memory.

Why do we ask about RAM?

Local AI quality and resource use depend heavily on model size. Instead of asking a customer to choose among many Qwen variants, this wizard turns a simple RAM choice into a practical recommendation.

If the customer selects Not sure, the wizard uses a lightweight fallback and does not assume a high-end computer.
