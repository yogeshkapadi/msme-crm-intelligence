Why are two options disabled?

We have deliberately enabled only Local AI in this MVP. BYOK and managed cloud AI involve credentials, third-party data transmission, provider policies, security controls, cost management and additional technical infrastructure.

We would rather keep those capabilities disabled than expose your data or credentials through an incomplete integration.
