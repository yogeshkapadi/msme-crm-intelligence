🔐 AI disabled in this MVP. No CRM records are sent to an external AI provider.

What AI will eventually do

Once enabled with explicit customer consent, the analyst can explain trends, identify stalled opportunities, surface at-risk customers, summarize salesperson performance and recommend actions.

“Why did revenue decline this quarter?” → Explain key drivers
“Which customers need attention?” → Surface risk signals
“Where is my pipeline leaking?” → Identify stage conversion issues
“What should management do next?” → Generate prioritized actions

Privacy design

The first version keeps raw CRM data in the browser. AI will be an explicit opt-in capability with clear disclosure about what information is processed externally.
